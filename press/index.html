The Stage Show

Theatrical magic built for a stage and an audience out front. Large scale illusions, escape, and the pace of a real production rather than a set of tricks.

Theatres · corporate galas · performing arts centres
Up to 1,000

Houdini: The Centennial Show

The real story of Houdini told by a historian who has spent thirty years on it, with escapes performed live from ropes, chains and handcuffs. Houdini died on 31 October 1926; the centennial year runs to 31 October 2027.

Theatres · museums · festivals · historical societies
Any size

The Houdini Lecture

The history programme. His escapes, his failures, the people who shaped him, and how he really did it. Needs no staging, only a room and an audience.

Libraries · universities · historical societies · museums
Keynote

The Houdini Way

How Houdini built worldwide fame with no internet, no mass media and no budget, and what an ordinary person can take from it. Real historical research, applied to the business world, by a working magic historian.

Conferences · sales meetings · associations

The Astonify Show

Victorian parlour magic and a seance, written for adults. Close enough that an audience can see everything, in the format the Victorians actually watched it in.
